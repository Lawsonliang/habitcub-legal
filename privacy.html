Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

1. Scope

This policy covers the habitcub app for iPhone and iPad (the app). The app is set up and managed by a parent or guardian, who uses it together with their child. Children do not sign up on their own.

The app stores your profile and activity data locally, with no account system or server of ours. Apple StoreKit may use the network for subscription products, purchases, and restoring purchases. If you choose to send feedback by email, that email leaves your device.

2. What We Collect

We do not automatically receive your profile or activity data.

There is no account, so we do not ask for an email address, a username, or an account password to use the app. The app includes no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service. If you send feedback, we receive the email and any information you choose to include, as described in section 6. Apple processes subscription transactions as described in section 5.

3. Data on Your Device

To work, the app saves some information locally: the child's first name, age, optional gender, the goals you create, check-in records, points, achievements, privileges, and in-app messages, plus a local count of how many goals you created during the free trial (so deleting a goal does not restore a trial slot). The system keychain stores the vendor identifier iOS supplies and the date your free trial began, to prevent reinstalling the app from restarting the trial. It also stores an entitlement cache (subscription expiry, grace-period deadline, last verification time, and the start of a period when verification is unavailable) and the last entitlement state, so access can be determined across app launches. These local records are not uploaded to us.

Under Apple's definition, data that stays on the device and is never sent to the developer is not collected. That is why our App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

4. How We Store It

Profile and activity data are kept in the app's own sandboxed storage on your device; the items described in section 3 use the system keychain. The parent password is stored only as a salted hash and can never be read back as text. Local storage does not mean that Apple subscription services or email feedback work without a network connection.

5. Subscriptions

The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to request subscription products, make purchases, restore purchases, and read subscription status. These Apple services may require a network connection. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card, billing address, or Apple Account details. The app receives product information and subscription results, including the product identifier, validity, trial status, and expiry date, to determine access locally. To cancel or change a subscription, use your Apple Account subscription settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Apple handles subscription transactions through StoreKit. Separately, the feedback screen opens your own email app with a prepared draft containing your feedback type, message, and any contact details you entered. That draft already contains the app version and the platform, so we can reproduce what you describe; you can delete that line, or the whole draft, before sending. Whatever you send reaches us, and we use it only to answer you.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is set up and managed by a parent or guardian. The app does not automatically send the child's profile or activity data to us. A parent who sends feedback should avoid including unnecessary personal information about the child. The child's first name, age, and optional gender stay on the parent's device and are used only to make goals and points age-appropriate. Parent password protection keeps a child from changing goals, privileges, or penalty settings.

8. Your Choices

You can review and change the profile, goals, privileges, and penalty settings inside the app at any time. You can delete any goal or privilege you created. Deleting the app removes its sandboxed data, but the keychain records described in section 3 may remain after deletion. The in-app clear-local-data function clears profile and activity data and the entitlement cache and state, but retains the trial identifier, trial start date, and trial usage count to prevent resetting the free trial. Deleting the app does not delete feedback emails you have sent us or cancel an Apple subscription. For questions about information you sent us, email us.

9. Retention

Profile and activity data stay in local app storage until removed in the app or by deleting the app. Keychain records may remain after app deletion; section 8 explains which records the clear-local-data function retains. Feedback emails you send us are separate from local app storage and are not removed by deleting the app. Apple manages its subscription and payment records separately.

10. Policy Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and the date at the top is updated. Material changes will be shown before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: sliang2012@163.com. We reply within 15 business days.
