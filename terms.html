Subscription Terms

Last updated: 2026-11-25

habitcub is free to use. An optional auto-renewable subscription (“habitcub Pro”) unlocks the penalty feature and raises the goal limit. This page explains the terms of that subscription. The app is licensed, not sold, under Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement, which governs your use of the app itself.

1. Plans and prices

Available plans, their length, and their price are shown in the app before you buy, on the membership screen. The price that applies is the one the App Store displays at the moment of purchase, in your local currency. Prices may change; a change never applies to a period you have already paid for.

2. Free trial

If a plan includes a free trial, its length is shown in the app before you buy. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it converts automatically into a paid subscription at the price shown.

3. Auto-renewal and billing

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.

4. Cancelling

To cancel, open Settings on your device, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select habitcub, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

5. Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall the app or use another device signed in to the same Apple Account, use the restore option on the membership screen to restore an active subscription.

6. Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot issue refunds ourselves.
